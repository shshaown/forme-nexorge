SUPPORT

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Can I buy this chair?+

This is an original portfolio concept. Pricing, stock and checkout are not available.

Are these real adjustment ranges?+

The controls demonstrate a design idea, not certified product specifications. Confirm all ranges with a manufacturer before production or purchase.

How do I explore the chair?+

Drag the chair or use left/right arrow keys to rotate. Click a part or its named button to highlight it. Use the sliders to recline and raise the seat.

Can I see a gaming setup?+

Select Gaming desk in the viewer to add a desk, monitor and keyboard to the live 3D scene.

Can I save my finish?+

Choose your finish on Make it yours and create a downloadable setup brief. Nothing is sent to a server.

What about delivery and warranty?+

Delivery, returns and warranty terms would need to be supplied by the eventual seller. No commercial promises are made in this concept.
